Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

CodeForge respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

  • Display names entered when creating or joining rooms.
  • Technical information such as browser type, IP address, and device information.
  • Room activity required to provide collaborative coding features.
  • Information collected by advertising providers such as Google AdSense.

Code and Room Data

CodeForge temporarily stores room data required to synchronize collaborative sessions. Room data may be deleted when sessions end.

WebRTC Video and Audio

Video and audio communication uses WebRTC technology. Camera and microphone access is only used when you grant permission through your browser.

Cookies

CodeForge and third-party providers may use cookies to improve functionality, analyze traffic, and display relevant advertisements.

Advertising

We may display advertisements through Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These providers may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads.

Third-Party Services

CodeForge may use third-party services including cloud providers, analytics providers, code execution services, and advertising networks.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent through our Contact page.
